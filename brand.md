# Brand Guidelines: Mahmoud Ayman (Timo)

## 1. Core Identity
- **Name**: Mahmoud Ayman (محمود أيمن)
- **Nickname**: Timo (تيمو)
- **Role**: Creative Graphic Designer (مصمم جرافيك كريتيف)
- **Experience**: 2 years
- **Philosophy**: Ideas you see before you read them. (أفكار تتشاف قبل ما تتقري). Every service starts with an idea. (كل خدمة تبدأ بفكرة).

## 2. Color Palette (Strict - No deviations)
- **Bordo**: `#6C151E`
  - Used for: Headings, accent blocks, tilted pill labels, active states.
  - Text on Bordo: Cream (`#F5DABF`).
  - Never place Green text on Bordo.
- **Green**: `#0F3D3A`
  - Used for: Body text on Cream background, dark immersive sections, dark buttons.
  - Text on Green: Cream (`#F5DABF`).
  - Never place Bordo text on Green.
- **Cream**: `#F5DABF`
  - Used for: Primary background, light typography on dark Bordo/Green surfaces.
- **Rules**:
  - NO pure black (`#000000`).
  - NO pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
  - NO new hues outside this primary trio.

## 3. Typography
- **Arabic**: Cairo (Google Fonts)
  - Headings: `Cairo Black (900)` / `ExtraBold (800)`
  - Subheadings / Body: `Cairo SemiBold (600)` / `Regular (400)`
- **English**: Space Grotesk (Google Fonts)
  - Bold, punchy editorial feel
  - Tilted pill labels, badges, technical details

## 4. Visual Language & Editorial Tone
- Bold oversized Arabic typography with tight negative space.
- Solid color blocks sliding and overlapping.
- Small tilted pill labels (`-2deg` to `3deg`) with English micro-labels.
- Confidence, editorial precision, black & white / duotone photography.
- Smooth GSAP & Lenis momentum scrolling.
